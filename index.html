About Eden

A Strong Start, Built on Excellence

The Eden School is a Cambridge International School in East Legon, Accra. Our crest carries the motto Excellentia Academica — a promise of academic excellence for every child who joins us.

From a child’s first days in nursery to the confidence of secondary school, we keep learning joyful, structured and personal.
